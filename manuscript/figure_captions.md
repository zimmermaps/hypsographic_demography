# Main figure captions

## Figure 1. Hypsographic demographic summary.

(A) Population in 2015 and 2025 by elevation group, using 2025 elevation classes held fixed across the interval. (B) Population age composition in 2025, showing the shares ages 0-14, 15-64, and 65+. (C) Population growth from 2015 to 2025 by elevation group. Low elevations contain most of the global population, while inhabited highlands are younger and faster-growing.

## Figure 2. Elevation and settlement context.

(A) Population distribution in 2025 by elevation group and settlement class. (B) Youth share in 2025 by elevation group and settlement class. (C) Population growth from 2015 to 2025 by elevation group and settlement class, using 2025 settlement classes held fixed across the interval. All cells are shown; the underlying table reports the 2015 population denominator for each growth cell. Settlement context reveals where highland demographic structure and growth are concentrated.
