Dataset S2. Robustness checks, sensitivity analyses, and comparisons

This dataset provides robustness checks and supporting comparisons for "Hypsographic Demography Revisited: Age Structure and Population Change by Elevation."

Files

1. dataset_s2_primary_robustness_checks.csv
   Primary robustness checks referenced in the manuscript, including alternative elevation thresholds, dependency-ratio summaries, and settlement-context comparisons.

2. dataset_s2_dynamic_vs_static2025_settlement.csv
   Comparison of static 2025 settlement-class summaries with epoch-specific, dynamic settlement-class summaries.

3. dataset_s2_country_highland_lowland.csv
   Country-level lowland and highland population, age-structure, and growth summaries.

4. dataset_s2_concentration_summary.csv
   Summary of countries contributing large highland populations or highland population change.

5. dataset_s2_leave_one_country_out.csv
   Full leave-one-country-out sensitivity results, excluding each country individually.

6. dataset_s2_leave_one_country_out_summary.csv
   Compact summary of leave-one-country-out results.

7. dataset_s2_country_exclusion_sensitivity.csv
   Targeted exclusion checks for countries discussed in the manuscript, including Ethiopia, Mexico, Kenya, Yemen, and Afghanistan.

8. dataset_s2_country_exclusion_ingredients.csv
   Ingredient table used to generate targeted country-exclusion sensitivity checks.

9. dataset_s2_leave_one_region_out.csv
   Leave-one-region-out sensitivity results.

10. dataset_s2_country_standardized_comparison.csv
    Country-standardized comparison of lowland and highland patterns.

11. dataset_s2_worldpop_metadata_status.csv
    Metadata and source-status notes for WorldPop inputs used in selected country summaries.

12. dataset_s2_country_decomposition_quality_checks.csv
    Quality checks for country-level decomposition and aggregation consistency.

Notes

- Dataset S2 supports robustness statements in the manuscript, including checks for aggregation choices, country composition, regional exclusions, alternative thresholds, country-standardized comparisons, dependency-ratio summaries, and static versus dynamic settlement classifications.
- Values are derived from modeled gridded population estimates and should be interpreted as aggregate modeled patterns, not direct evidence of mechanisms.
